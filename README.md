# Kingdom Learning Hub 👑📚

**One home for every learning app** — math, English, reading, spelling, school-readiness and study tools for the champions. Tap a card to open any app; install it to your home screen so the whole menu is one tap away.

> *"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."* — Proverbs 22:6 (KJV)

## What's inside
A single installable page that links out to the family's learning apps, grouped by subject:

- **📐 Math** — Summit Math 8, Kingdom Kids Math, Solid Ground
- **📖 English · Reading · Spelling** — Summit English 8, Kingdom Words, Kingdom Speller, Spell & Stack, Anu's Reading World / Words / Words+, Read & Cook
- **🎓 All-subjects** — Kingdom Kids Academy, Kids Learning Arcade, Anu Can!
- **🏫 School-readiness** — Sound Steps, Steady Steps, Morning Marchers, Get Ready Champions
- **📚 Study & exam prep** — Study Lab, Marvelous Hub, Called to Care, HESI A2 Prep (v1 & v2)

Search box + category chips make any app easy to find. Works offline (service worker), installable on phones and tablets ("Add to Home Screen").

## Editing the list
All apps live in the `GROUPS` array near the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`. Add, remove or re-group entries there, then **bump the `CACHE` version in `sw.js`** so installed devices pick up the change.
